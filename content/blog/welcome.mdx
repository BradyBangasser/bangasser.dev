This site is built to make it easy to write. Every post here is a single
Markdown/MDX file with a small frontmatter block at the top — `title`,
`date`, `summary`, `tags`, and `type` (`short` or `long`).

Short posts, like this one, are for quick notes: something I learned,
a small tool I built, a link with commentary. Long posts get a table of
contents and are meant for deeper technical write-ups.

To publish, drop a new `.mdx` file in `content/blog/` and it shows up
automatically — no database, no CMS.
